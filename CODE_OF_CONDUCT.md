# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expectations

* Participate in an authentic and active way.
* Exercise consideration and respect in your speech and actions.
* Attempt collaboration before conflict.
* Refrain from demeaning, discriminatory, or harassing behavior and speech.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers and will result in a ban from the repository.
